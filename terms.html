2. Acreage Licensing & Workspaces

Farmora licenses access to its platform based on contracted arable acreage or hectare blocks under active monitoring. Customer may register field boundaries, assign estate managers, agronomists, and scout operators up to the user tier designated in their active enterprise plan.

Season Hectare Reallocation

Agribusinesses operating multi-crop rotation cycles (e.g., wet season maize transitioning into dry season irrigated vegetables) may reallocate contracted hectare quotas between geographic sectors twice per calendar year without penalty.

3. Machine & Drone Connectivity

Customer is solely responsible for maintaining legitimate software licenses and physical access rights to any connected farm equipment (including John Deere Operations Center, DJI Agras drone fleets, Trimble Ag, and IoT soil probe telemetry). Farmora provides standardized ISO-XML and Shapefile (.SHP) variable-rate prescriptions formatted according to international agricultural electronics standards.

4. AI Decision-Support Guidance

Farmora’s spectral analytics, fungal disease detection, and soil moisture anomaly models are sophisticated decision-support instruments designed to assist certified agronomists and estate directors. While Farmora strives for maximum mathematical and remote-sensing accuracy, final decisions regarding chemical application volumes, irrigation pump schedules, and harvest decisions remain under the professional direction of Customer's authorized agronomic personnel.

5. Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Season Support

Farmora commits to a 99.9% uptime availability for its spatial query API and console ingestion engine during active regional growing seasons. In the event of emergency pest or disease outbreaks requiring urgent prescription generation, Farmora's Lagos-based agronomic engineering operations team provides priority 2-hour response times for enterprise tier customers.

6. Trade Secrets & Commercial Yield Data

Farmora recognizes that crop yield forecasts, harvested tonnages, outgrower payment registers, and parcel spatial boundaries are highly sensitive commercial trade secrets. Farmora maintains strict non-disclosure obligations, and under no circumstances will Farmora disclose Customer's yield figures to third-party grain traders, commodity exchanges, or financial institutions without explicit written authorization.
