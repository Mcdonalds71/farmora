Transforming 3-day manual field walks into 3-minute continuous telemetry.

The traditional agronomy workflow is reactive. By the time visual symptoms appear on leaves, yield loss is already irreversible. Farmora detects stress sub-surface, autonomously.

The Problem: Reactive Scouting

Commercial agribusinesses lose 15-20% of yield annually simply because agronomists cannot physically walk 5,000 hectares every day. Nitrogen deficits and early blight multiply unseen until physical wilting begins.

The Solution: Autonomous Field Intelligence

Farmora ingests satellite imagery, soil probe telemetry, and microclimate data hourly. Our proprietary AI isolates chlorosis and moisture stress weeks before physical wilting, instantly routing drones or center pivots to correct the anomaly.
