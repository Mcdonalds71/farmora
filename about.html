OUR COMPANY

Built by agronomists and AI researchers in Nigeria.

We know what happens when rain patterns shift and inputs are wasted. Farmora exists to secure African and global food systems through deterministic AI.

Agronomy first.
Technology second.

We didn't start in a Silicon Valley garage. Farmora was born out of the necessity to manage sprawling commercial estates in Northern Nigeria, where connectivity is sparse and the cost of nitrogen fertilizer can break a season's margin.

Our hardware and ML pipelines are built to be extremely resilient, optimizing every drop of water and kilogram of input to protect both commercial yields and local soil ecology.

Field-Tested Resilience

Designed for low-connectivity, high-heat environments. If it works in the Sahel, it works anywhere.
