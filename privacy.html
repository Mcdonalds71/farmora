Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Farmora, Inc. ("Farmora", "we", "us") provides an AI-assisted platform that helps growers and agribusiness teams monitor field health and forecast yield. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, work email, organization name, and role.
  • Field & farm data you connect — field boundaries, satellite and drone imagery, and yield history.
  • Usage data — how you interact with the product, used to improve reliability and features.
  • Technical data — device, browser, and log information collected to keep the service secure.

How we use your information

  • To score field health and detect pest, disease, and irrigation issues.
  • To generate yield forecasts for your connected fields.
  • To operate, secure, maintain, and improve the platform.
  • To communicate about your account, updates, and support.

Data protection & security

Your farm data is yours. Each organization's workspace is isolated, encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not sell your data, and we do not use one organization's field data to train models shared with another.

You are responsible for having the appropriate rights to connect the field and imagery data you upload to Farmora. We apply role-based access controls, audit logging, and least-privilege practices across our infrastructure.

AI processing

Field health scoring, yield prediction, and pest/disease detection are performed by AI models running on our secured cloud infrastructure. Scores and forecasts are decision-support — your team is responsible for final agronomic decisions.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help us operate the platform (such as cloud hosting, satellite imagery providers, and analytics), under contracts that require them to protect it, and where required by law.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email privacy@farmora.io or write to Farmora, Inc., Data Protection Office.
